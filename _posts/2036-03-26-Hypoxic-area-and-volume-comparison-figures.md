As part of the intermodel comparison discussion we would like to take a closer look at the hypoxic area and volume by subinlets in the Puget Sound. This is motivated by the question of where and when the most hypoxia occurs-- the timing in some inlets may be out of phase with the main basins. 

Aurora has created a script to run a timeseries of hypoxic volume from 2014-2020. This reads in a box extraction (using `LO/extract/box`) that effectively crops the raw LiveOcean output to a smaller domain to make these (still rather large) files easier to work with. Aurora has also further processed the files into `[gtagex]_pugetsoundDO_[year]_DO_info.nc. These contain time-variable bottom DO concentration, thickness of the water column, and thickness of the water column that is <= 2 mg/L, <= 1 mg/L and <= 3 mg/L.
