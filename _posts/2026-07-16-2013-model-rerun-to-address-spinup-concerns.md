## 2013 model rerun to address spinup concerns
We reran the year 2013 on itself twice (for a total of three repeat 2013 years), starting each run with an initial condition at the end of the previous year. LiveOcean is initialized in fall 2012, with 2013 considered a spin-up year. Looking at timeseries of dissolved oxygen and nitrate we are concerned that there are still spin-up effects in 2014-- nitrate seemed to continue accumulating and DO decreased particularly in hood canal through 2014 (Figure 1).
