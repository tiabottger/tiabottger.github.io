## Boundary condition in the Strait of Juan de Fuca
<img width="5000" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0f39d9d0-a2c4-4500-b04d-a1e094771a68" />
Figure 2: Bottom cell values extracted at the Strait of Juan de Fuca for cas7_t1_x11ab and cas7_t1y14_x11ab. 

We are providing the boundary condition files to SalishSeaCast from cas7_t1_x11ab. We were curious to see how different the boundary condition in the Strait of Juan de Fuca is for our more spun-up version of 2014 (cas7_t1y14_x11ab). There is overall slightly less oxygen and slightly more nitrate in our rerun version of 2014. Note the axes are more zoomed in than Figure 1, so the differences are slighter in the Strait. Interestingly, after September the versions of the model reverse and the original 2014 run has slightly less oxygen and slightly more nitrate.
