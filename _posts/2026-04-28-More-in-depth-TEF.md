## Eulerian vs. Salt flux decomposition
In the classic Eulerian view, salt flux through the section is computed as (tidally averaged):
$F = \left\langle \int us dA \right\rangle$ 

Salinity space identifies multiple inflow/outflow layers as multiple extrema in Q(S). In Eulerian space identifying multiple layers of inflow and outflow is subjective.
